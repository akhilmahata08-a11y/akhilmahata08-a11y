## How I work with clients

1. **Brief** — you tell me the goal, the audience and any existing brand material.
2. **Scope & quote** — you get a written scope, a fixed price and a delivery date before anything starts.
3. **Build** — I work in short cycles and share a live preview link so you can see progress, not just screenshots.
4. **Review** — two rounds of revisions are included; anything beyond that is quoted separately.
5. **Launch & handover** — deployment, a short walkthrough, and the source code in your own GitHub account.
